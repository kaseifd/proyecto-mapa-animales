About the Atlas

Explore the Wild

A world of wildlife waiting to be explored. Discover the diversity of the animal kingdom, from familiar species to lesser-known creatures, and explore the habitats they call home. An interactive journey designed to spark curiosity and bring us closer to the natural world.

Behind the Project

Developed as a web development project, this interactive wildlife atlas combines HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and external APIs to create an engaging and educational experience.

The website allows users to discover animal species from around the world through an interactive map. By selecting a continent or ocean, users can explore a list of animals and access detailed information about each species, including its habitat, geographical distribution, and conservation status.

A search bar and filtering options make it easier to find specific animals, while individual information cards provide a clear and visual way to explore each species.

The project's goal is to make global biodiversity more accessible and encourage curiosity about wildlife and its conservation.
